# Community artwork

Curated from the public [ASCII Motion community](https://ascii-motion.app/community), using the site's public gallery exports. On 2026-10-14, all three project records had `allow_remixes: true`. The site's [Terms of Service, section 4.3](https://github.com/CameronFoxly/Ascii-Motion/blob/main/docs/TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md#43-public-sharing-future-feature) grants users the right to view and remix published projects. The creators retain ownership of their artwork; the editor's MIT license is separate from these community assets.

The original character grids are preserved, with a monochrome palette. Birds retain their original IBM VGA typography; the other two use locally hosted JetBrains Mono. The squirrel's blue background dot field is removed and empty margins cropped. The abstract texture uses every third original frame while retaining its approximate duration. Birds play more slowly, once on footer entry, through their original final empty frame. Large editor session metadata and unused UI settings are excluded from the compact assets. No editor package, React dependency, or external runtime connection is needed.
