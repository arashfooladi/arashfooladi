# Community artwork

Birds and Squirrel are curated from the public [ASCII Motion community](https://ascii-motion.app/community), using the site's public gallery exports. On 2026-10-14, both project records had `allow_remixes: true`. The site's [Terms of Service, section 4.3](https://github.com/CameronFoxly/Ascii-Motion/blob/main/docs/TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md#43-public-sharing-future-feature) grants users the right to view and remix published projects. The creators retain ownership of their artwork; the editor's MIT license is separate from these community assets.

| Asset | Artist | Original |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `birds.json` | CameronFoxly | [Birds](https://ascii-motion.app/community/project/d0a249d8-acf0-4e81-939b-18053b5cdc3e) |
| `squirrel.json` | CameronFoxly | [Squirrel](https://ascii-motion.app/community/project/5097d8dd-9b38-4786-b7dd-dcb18754584d) |

The original character grids are preserved, with a monochrome palette. Birds retain their original IBM VGA typography; Squirrel uses locally hosted JetBrains Mono. The squirrel's blue background dot field is removed and empty margins cropped. Birds play more slowly, once on footer entry, through their original final empty frame. Large editor session metadata and unused UI settings are excluded from the compact assets. No editor package, React dependency, or external runtime connection is needed.

`world-map.json` is a local character rendering of Natural Earth's 1:110m land polygons from the [official dataset repository](https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson). Natural Earth data is [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/). The map has no country borders or political labels; the coastline grid receives a short, local shimmer animation.

`telegram.txt` is a character-grid rendering of the [official Telegram logo](https://telegram.org/img/t_logo.svg), used for the personal Telegram contact link. Telegram's [press information](https://telegram.org/press#telegram-logos) allows logos for this kind of link/button use. The site is the author's personal portfolio, with no suggestion of official Telegram representation.
